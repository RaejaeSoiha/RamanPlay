# Viewing-source policy

A source must be on an approved HTTPS domain and have game-specific evidence. Prefer official listings that name the exact matchup, kickoff, region, and access terms. Do not derive entitlement just from the network name. Keep evidence in `notes`, supply the precise region, and use a canonical official URL without account/session tokens.

Labels:
- FREE: `FREE_LEGAL` video explicitly available without payment. A login, region limit, or device restriction must be described. Do not label cable-authenticated access free.
- FREE TRIAL: requires enrollment and may auto-renew; spell out eligibility and current terms.
- SUBSCRIPTION: paid plan, cable authentication, or equivalent required.
- OFFICIAL: official information/directory, without claiming video availability.
- AUDIO ONLY: listening, not video; note any payment requirement.
- UNAVAILABLE: source cannot be used; HTTP failures are additionally shown as availability badges.

A FREE source is a claim entered by the trusted feed owner. The checker verifies URL safety/reachability, not the truth of broadcaster rights or terms. Reconfirm claims before using them. No free source is seeded. The Peacock source is marked DEVELOPMENT EXAMPLE and is not entitlement evidence for a real game.

References checked September 11, 2026:
- https://www.nfl.com/ways-to-watch — location/service-specific official directory.
- https://support.nfl.com/hc/en-us/articles/40568374746644-How-to-access-LIVE-NFL-Regular-Season-games — device and provider restrictions.

No unauthorized streams, mirror hunting, embedding, downloading, paywall bypasses, geo-block bypasses, or DRM handling are implemented.
